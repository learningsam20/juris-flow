## Talking points (not on slides)

**Product in one breath**  
JurisFlow = knowledge hub + grounded contract review + multi-agent scenario play + training artefacts.

**How it earns trust**
- Findings without KM support stay *manual-review* — no invented “risk”
- Same KM trains the sim *and* confirms the audit
- HITL: pause / inject / steer / approve — humans gate judgment, not every clause
- Swap Ollama ↔ Vertex without rewriting the product

**Scope discipline**
- Assist and rehearse — do not replace counsel
- KM artefacts ≠ contracts under review
- Educational disclaimers on exports and audio
