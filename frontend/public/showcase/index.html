JurisFlow Enterprise AI · Final presentation

Slide 1 · Why this problem

Enterprise legal work still runs on scarce experts.

Theme: enterprise AI — where proprietary knowledge, high stakes, and human judgment must stay in the loop.

Need

Review contracts, train juniors, stay current on policy — at speed and scale.

Challenge

SMEs are the bottleneck: insights are manual, inconsistent, slow; repetition burns judgment time.

Why enterprise AI

Proprietary knowledge, high stakes, HITL required — legal KM is the natural fit.

What we chose

Own the firm’s knowledge; automate grounded audits and immersive tribunal training around it.

Outcome: free SME bandwidth for judgment — scale review and rehearsal without waiting on experts.

Slide 2 · How it works

System architecture

Policy-gated API, knowledge hub, grounded review, and LangGraph agents — LLM adapters swap without lock-in.

AI in the loop

  • RAG — KM + dossiers ground review, Ask AI, sim turns
  • Agents — plaintiff / defendant / judge; LLM-as-judge verdict
  • Generate — remediations, case studies, dual-track TTS

Tech bets

  • Agnostic — Ollama ↔ Vertex via config, no lock-in
  • FOSS — FastAPI · React · LangGraph · Qdrant · OPA
  • Policy-first — Rego on API, MCP, exports
